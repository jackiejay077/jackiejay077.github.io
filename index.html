About

I evaluate how generative AI systems interpret intent, context, and risk—especially when meaning emerges across multiple turns or inputs rather than from one trigger phrase.

My work sits at the seam between adversarial testing and operations: design the evaluation, inspect the judgment, isolate the failure pattern, and build the quality layer that keeps the same error from quietly becoming normal.
